# Parasol demo video: script

Voice: Kokoro TTS `af_heart` (female). 1920x1080, length 2:56.5.
Opens on the albinism story (UN stat card), then Sam's walkthrough; Ada (deep skin) is the second example.
Footer on footage scenes: "Demo faces and campaign images are AI-generated. Not medical advice." AI faces are labelled "AI-generated demo face".
Subtitles: `docs/parasol-demo.srt`.

## 1. Hook / problem (UN stat card) (0:00.8)

> The United Nations says that in some African countries, most people with albinism die of skin cancer between thirty and forty. And every new city does something new to your skin: the sun, the heat, the dry plane air.

## 2. Title (0:14.4)

> So I built Parasol: a day-by-day skin forecast for every skin, from albinism to deep skin.

## 3. Home (phone + desktop) (0:21.1)

> One selfie, any trip. Scan your own face, or try the demo with no selfie. The demo faces are AI-generated, and the app says so.

## 4. Sam's scan: YouCam read type V, Parasol uses type I (highlighted) (0:30.3)

> This is Sam. She has albinism and very fair skin. YouCam reads her skin type, zone by zone, and her sun type. On her face it read type five. Parasol shows that honestly, then uses type one, the most careful setting, because she has albinism.

## 5. Trip (0:46.7)

> Now the trip: Cairo, then Dubai, then Reykjavik, with the real weather for every day.

## 6. Forecast + burn clock + Show details (0:52.2)

> Each day gets a forecast for Sam's skin: heat, humidity, cold, dry cabin air. And a burn clock: about how many minutes before she may start to burn. Always an estimate, never medical advice.

## 7. Kit + sun protection extras (1:05.2)

> Then her kit. Thirty-two real products, from brands like Elta MD, CeraVe and Supergoop. Every pick says why it's for her: SPF fifty, fragrance-free, carry-on size. Then the sun protection extras: a hat, sleeves, and UV four hundred sunglasses.

## 8. Shopify checkout (test payment) (1:23.0)

> Checkout runs on a real Shopify store, with a test payment, so no real money moves.

## 9. Day 3 in Dubai (1:28.8)

> Now Ada, day three in Dubai. Her skin feels different, so she rescans in the hotel. Her acne score is twelve points worse than at home. The likely cause comes from the real weather since she landed: muggy heat. Then what to do tonight, and when to see a pharmacist.

## 10. Today: routine, sun timer, read aloud, low-vision mode (1:45.7)

> Every morning, Today gives the routine for today's weather. Tap I'm going out, and a sun timer counts down to the next reapply. Read aloud speaks the page. Low-vision mode makes the text big, with strong contrast or a gentle dark theme, because many people with albinism also have low vision.

## 11. Ada: deep skin (2:04.9)

> Ada has very deep skin. Parasol never tells her to skip sunscreen. Her advice is about dark marks the sun can leave for months, and her kit puts sunscreens with no white cast first.

## 12. Business model (2:16.8)

> Parasol is free for travellers. It earns a commission on each trip kit, and from hotels, airlines and pharmacies that offer it to their customers. Albinism charities get it free.

## 13. YouCam tools + market (2:28.4)

> Under it all is the YouCam API: AI Skin Analysis, the Fitzpatrick Scale Analyzer, Skin Simulation for Ada's day three photo, and AI Image Generator for the demo faces. YouCam already works with more than eight hundred brands. Parasol helps them sell sun care to both ends of the scale.

## 14. Close (2:47.9)

> Parasol. Your skin, on the road. Try it at parasol dash topaz dot vercel dot app.
